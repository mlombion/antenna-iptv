ANTENNA IPTV — live TV, movies and series on your Mac

INSTALL
1. Drag "Antenna IPTV" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Antenna IPTV from Applications or Launchpad.
   Needs macOS 14 or newer. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

LIVE TV
Sign in with the Xtream Codes details your IPTV provider gave you (server URL, username, password).
Your password stays in your Mac's Keychain.
- Double-click a channel (or press Return) to watch. Esc goes back.
- Up / Down: change channel. Type a number to jump to that channel.
- Left / Right: rewind or skip 10 seconds (hold Shift for 60). Space pauses live TV.
- P: picture in picture. F: full screen. Cmd-K: search.
If a channel won't play: Settings > Stream format > HLS.

MOVIES & SERIES (optional)
Sign in with your Stremio account (Facebook, Apple or email, on Stremio's own page) to use your library,
Continue Watching and installed add-ons. Progress is saved back to your account.
Torrent sources are streamed by Antenna itself: while you watch, your Mac downloads from and shares with
other people's computers, like any torrent app. Only play what you're allowed to in your country.

WHAT ANTENNA CONNECTS TO
Your IPTV provider; if you use Movies & Series, Stremio's account service, the add-ons you installed and,
for torrent sources, other torrent users. Antenna has no server and collects nothing.
